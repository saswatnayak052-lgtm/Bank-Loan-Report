# Bank-Loan-Report
End-to-end Bank Loan Analytics project using SQL and Power BI. Performed data cleaning, transformation, KPI analysis, and dashboard visualization to identify loan risk patterns, customer behavior, and business insights from 38K+ loan records.
## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing bank loan data to understand loan performance, risk distribution, and customer behavior. The dashboard provides insights into good vs bad loans, loan trends, and key factors influencing loan approval and repayment.

---

## 🎯 Objective
- Analyze loan application trends and funding patterns  
- Identify risk segments (Good Loans vs Bad Loans)  
- Understand customer behavior based on loan purpose, employment, and home ownership  
- Support data-driven decision-making in financial services  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- SQL (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- Power BI (Dashboard & Visualization)
- DAX (Measures & KPIs)
- CSV Dataset  

---

## ⚙️ Project Workflow
Data Collection → SQL Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → Power BI Modeling → Dashboard Creation → Insights Generation

---

## 📊 Key KPIs
- Total Loan Applications: **38.6K**
- Total Funded Amount: **$435.8M**
- Total Amount Received: **$473.1M**
- Average Interest Rate: **12.0%**
- Average DTI: **13.3%**
- Good Loan Ratio: **86.2%**
- Bad Loan Ratio: **13.8%**

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- A strong **86.2% of loans are classified as good loans**, indicating a stable and low-risk loan portfolio.
- **Bad loans account for 13.8%**, representing a significant risk segment that needs monitoring.
- Total received amount ($473.1M) is higher than funded amount ($435.8M), indicating **profitable lending operations**.
- Loan applications show a **steady upward trend throughout the year**, peaking in Q4 (Oct–Dec).
- Majority of loans are issued for **Debt Consolidation (~18K applications)**, making it the dominant loan purpose.
- Most customers prefer **60-month loan terms (~73%)**, indicating demand for long-term financing.
- Customers with **10+ years of employment contribute the highest number of applications**, indicating higher financial stability.
- **Rent and Mortgage categories dominate home ownership**, showing most borrowers do not fully own property.
- Loan grades and sub-grades help identify **risk distribution across different customer profiles**.

---

## 💡 Business Impact

- Helps banks identify **high-risk loan segments** (bad loans)
- Supports **better credit risk assessment**
- Enables **targeted lending strategies based on customer behavior**
- Improves **profitability by analyzing funded vs received amounts**
- Assists in **portfolio optimization and risk management**

---

## 🎯 Recommendations

- Reduce bad loan percentage by tightening credit policies for high-risk segments  
- Focus on high-performing loan categories like long-term stable customers  
- Monitor debt consolidation loans closely as they form the majority  
- Promote shorter-term loans to reduce long-term risk exposure  
- Improve customer profiling using loan grades and financial behavior

## 📈 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how data analytics can be used to evaluate loan performance, assess risk, and improve financial decision-making using SQL and Power BI.
